# Aviora — Demo Video Walkthrough Script

A step-by-step production script for recording the application demonstration video (Duration: 7 minutes, 10 seconds).

| Timestamp | Section | Visual Action | Voiceover Narration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **00:00 - 00:20** | Intro | Launch the app, showing the animated Aviora airplane logo on the splash screen transitioning to the login page. | "Welcome to Aviora, a real-time airport passenger assistance platform. Today we'll showcase the complete passenger journey and the operations staff dashboard. As you see, launching the app takes us through a smooth splash transition straight to our secure login interface." |
| **00:20 - 00:45** | Login Checks | Type in an invalid email (e.g., `test@com`) and show the inline validation error. Then enter a wrong password to show the Snackbar warning. | "Security and form validation are built into every field. When I type an invalid email format, the system highlights it with a red warning banner. Entering incorrect credentials runs a loading spinner and pops up a clear failure message." |
| **00:45 - 01:15** | Pax Signup | Tap 'Sign Up', create a new user profile with realistic data (Amit Patel), validate matching passwords, and click Register. | "Registering a new passenger is quick and secure. Our fields validate inputs on the fly—ensuring names are of proper length, phone numbers are exactly ten digits, and passwords match perfectly before unlocking the register action." |
| **01:15 - 01:45** | Dashboard Tour | Routes to Passenger Home. Tour the greeting header, statistical tiles, quick action keys, and empty active requests banner. | "Upon registration, passengers land on their personalized home dashboard. We are greeted with a time-appropriate message. The stats row tracks active and completed requests, and the grid provides quick actions to navigate the app." |
| **01:45 - 02:30** | Request Wizard | Click 'New Request' to open Step 1. Enter PNR (XYZ789), flight (AI-101), select airport and travel date. Progress to Step 2 to fill traveler info. | "Let's submit our first request. Step 1 gathers flight details, validating the PNR length and ensuring the flight date is in the future. In Step 2, we enter passenger contact details and select the required category of assistance." |
| **02:30 - 03:00** | Draft Restores | Close the form screen, go back to Home, and tap 'New Request' again to trigger the draft restore pop-up banner. | "Aviora protects travelers against data loss. If we accidentally leave the form, our background draft service autosaves progress. Reopening the form triggers a restoration banner letting us resume right where we left off." |
| **03:00 - 03:30** | Doc Uploads | Progress to Step 3, click document picker, upload a passport photo, show progress loaders, and tap submit. | "In Step 3, we upload passport copies or medical verifications. Supported files are limited to under 10MB. The progress bar updates, and tapping submit pushes our request to Firestore and purges our local drafts cache." |
| **03:30 - 04:00** | Track & QR | Open the tracking screen. Show the collapsible QR Code ticket and the pulsing 'New Request' node on the vertical status timeline. | "Our request is live! The tracking screen displays a collapsible QR ticket containing our request ID for quick airport scanning. The vertical status timeline shows a breathing pulse animation on our active milestone, 'New Request'." |
| **04:00 - 04:40** | Staff Login | Logout, log back in using staff credentials (`staff@aviora.com`). Show the Requests list dashboard populated with passenger Amit's request. | "Let's switch to the operations side. Logging in as support staff displays all active airport requests. The unassigned request from Amit Patel appears at the top, marked with a blue 'New Request' badge." |
| **04:40 - 05:20** | Search/Filters | Type 'AI-101' in the Searchbar. Tap the 'New Request' chip filter. Tap the card to route to the details panel. | "Operational efficiency is maintained with our search and filtering system. Staff can filter by status chips or search by flight numbers or PNR. Tapping the request card opens a comprehensive passenger details panel." |
| **05:20 - 06:00** | Staff Assignment | Click 'Assign Staff'. Select available staff Priya Sharma, show Dialog confirmation, and complete assignment. | "Let's assign support staff to this request. Tapping 'Assign Staff' lists only the personnel currently marked as active and available in the system. Selecting Priya Sharma triggers a confirmation dialog to prevent accidental clicks." |
| **06:00 - 06:30** | Gates & Chats | Open 'Update Status'. Progress to 'Passenger Contacted', add a note. Open the chat log, send a message to passenger Amit. | "With staff assigned, the request status moves to 'Staff Assigned' and Priya's status updates to busy. Status progression is strictly sequential to maintain protocol. Let's send a chat message to Amit to coordinate meeting locations." |
| **06:30 - 07:00** | Live Sync & Notif | Logout staff, log back in as passenger Amit. View the live updated timeline (Contacted) and read the message inside the chat window. | "Let's log back in as passenger Amit. We see a red unread badge overlay on our notification bell. Opening it shows the status update. In our chat logs, the message from staff is aligned on the left, updating in real-time." |
| **07:00 - 07:10** | Outro | Toggle Dark Mode in Settings. Tour the dark theme dashboard and conclude. | "Finally, passengers can toggle Dark Mode in settings to save battery life. Thank you for touring Aviora—a complete, grade-ready airport assistance application." |
